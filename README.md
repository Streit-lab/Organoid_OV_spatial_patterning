# Organoid_OV_spatial_patterning
R code for the reanalysis of scRNA-seq data from Human Inner Ear Organoids in: 
## Incomplete Spatial Patterning of Otic Vesicles in Human Inner Ear Organoids

Ana Garcia Urbano<sup>1</sup>, Jingyan Yang<sup>1</sup>, David J. Pearton<sup>1</sup>, Mizan Ahmad<sup>1</sup>, Graham Cocks<sup>2</sup>, Alexandre Thiery<sup>1</sup>, Tonglin Zheng<sup>3</sup>, Andrea Streit<sup>1</sup>

1.  Centre for Craniofacial and Regenerative Biology, Faculty of Dentistry, Oral and Craniofacial Science, King’s College London, London, UK
2.  Genome Editing and Embryology Core, King’s College London, London, UK
3. Computer Graphics, Vision and Imaging Group, Department of Computer Science, University College London, London, UK


The processed Seurat scRNA-seq object described in Steinhart et al., 2023 was downloaded from GEO (GSE188936) as an R object (RDS) and analysed in Seurat V5.

**Steinhart, M.R., van der Valk, W.H., Osorio, D., Serdy, S.A., Zhang, J., Nist-Lund, C., Kim, J., Moncada-Reid, C., Sun, L., Lee, J., Koehler, K.R.,** 2023. Mapping oto-pharyngeal development in a human inner ear organoid model. *Development* 150.
